ভরসা/bʱɔr·ʃa/

noun. From Sanskrit; shared with Hindi and Urdu bharosa.

  1. Trust. Belief that someone is honest and will not let you down.
  2. Reliance. The person or thing you depend on when you cannot manage alone.
  3. Hope, assurance. The steadiness that lets you take the next step.

Why this name

Going abroad for treatment is hard enough. You should not have to do it alone.

When a family in Dhaka, Chattogram or Sylhet decides on surgery in India, the worry is rarely the doctor. It is everything around the doctor: which hospital, which papers, where to stay, how to talk to the nurses, what it will really cost. In Bangla, the person who carries that weight with you is your ভরসা.

That is the job we have given ourselves.
